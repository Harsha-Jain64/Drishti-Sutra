CASE DS-003 - VENDOR PAYMENT DISPUTE (SYNTHETIC TRAINING CASE)

Parties: Neha Verma (buyer) and Arjun Rao (vendor). All names, numbers and documents in this folder are fictional.

Background: The vendor issued an invoice for the October batch. The buyer states the invoice was settled in two
payments, an advance and a balance. The vendor disputes the amount of the balance. Each side supplied different
records: identity documents, a chat screenshot, a chat export, and a device event log.

Scope of review: compare the records for internal and cross-source consistency. Findings are indicators for human
review and are not conclusions about any person.
